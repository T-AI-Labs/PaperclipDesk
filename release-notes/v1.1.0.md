# PaperclipDesk 1.1.0

- Several connections stay open at once: remotes side by side, next to the local server. The **Connect** menu switches between them and marks the open ones.
- The connection page shows every connection as a card; remotes and what opens at launch are managed under **Settings → Connections**.
- The menu bar follows the app's design and colour scheme.
- The settings use the type sizes of Paperclip and list their pages by subject.
- New: an About dialog, links to the project and a way to report a problem, and a switch for the update check at launch.
