# PaperclipDesk 1.2.0

- A menu bar replaces the sidebar: switch companies and reach Paperclip's main pages and actions from the Company, New, Go to and Help menus. Every menu action has a keyboard shortcut, and the menu bar can be hidden.
- The settings sit behind the gear at the right of the menu bar, with a search field; the local server, your remotes, updates and the keyboard shortcuts open as panels over Paperclip.
- Company from template: pick one of the ready-made companies of Paperclip's catalog, see its agents, hierarchy, teams and skills, choose the AI its agents work with, and create it. It works before any server runs.
- The notification bell is back and shows what waits in Paperclip's inbox for the company in front.
- Newer Paperclip servers can be installed from the server panel, from a list the project signs, with a backup of the data first.
- A welcome page opens when no connection is chosen to open at start, and Paperclip follows the app's colour scheme.
